# Result-screen civil engineering trivia

The result screen introduces one short, source-linked civil engineering fact at a time. Players can turn to the next fact, and the first fact advances after each match. This gives returning players something new to read without interrupting the game loop. A portrait rendered from the game's existing Bot GLB serves as the guide, keeping the feature within INFRA RUSH's visual world.

The facts describe real infrastructure, not the game's simplified bridge health or construction rules. The twelve cards span excavation, bulldozing, motor grading, embankment construction and drainage, crushed stone, reuse of excavated soil, bridge launching and repair, seismic reinforcement, and bridge inspection. Bridge facts are interleaved with earthwork and machinery facts so the same topic does not dominate successive results. Wording was checked against the linked publications by Japan's Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism. Each in-game card links to its source so players can read further. The bridge inspection facts use the [Road Bridge Periodic Inspection Manual](https://www.mlit.go.jp/road/sisaku/yobohozen/tenken/yobo7_23.pdf); the other sources are recorded beside the individual facts in `src/content/trivia.ts`.

On narrow screens, the result summary is condensed so the Bot and fact appear early, with the replay controls immediately below. A 320px-wide screen can still scroll vertically; it does not scroll horizontally.

Each fact now has a matching model portrait rendered from the production GLB: machinery for excavation and grading, earth and stone for materials, and bridges or the launcher for bridge work. The Bot remains a small guide in the scene. Transparent PNG portraits keep the result card quick to load and avoid a second WebGL renderer; `assets/blender/render_trivia_models.py` recreates them from the current models. The card gives the model a dedicated stage and keeps the explanation in a separate, readable panel.
