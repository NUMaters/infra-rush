# Title mode selection

The title now offers **シングルプレイ** and **マルチプレイ** as the two entry points. Selecting single player opens a second choice: **チュートリアル** or **CPU戦**. This follows the requested distinction between learning the controls and starting a match immediately.

The tutorial still starts a fresh CPU match after completion or when skipped. Choosing CPU戦 skips practice but keeps the team introduction and countdown. Difficulty is selected on the single-player choice screen and applies to either route. Returning to the title resets the menu to the two main modes.
